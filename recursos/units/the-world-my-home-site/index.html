🦆 Doña Tina: The Guardian of the Tibanica Wetland

📍 Orientation

Mrs. Tina is an environmental leader from Bosa. She is known as the guardian of the Tibanica Wetland.

🌱 Early Life

She spent her childhood in the countryside of Boyacá, surrounded by animals and nature.

🗓️ Beginning

In 1994, she arrived in Bosa, joined the local environmental committee, trained and developed her skills.

💧 Current Actions

She works to raise awareness, restore the wetland's water and help birds return.

✍️ Grammar Power: Imperatives + Should / Shouldn't

📣 Imperatives

Use them to give instructions.

Recycle paper. Protect animals. Don't throw trash.

💡 Should / Shouldn't

Use them to give recommendations.

You should save water. You shouldn't pollute wetlands.
